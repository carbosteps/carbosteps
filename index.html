Technical breakdown

Why It Works.

Carbon provides the support

A thin carbon structure sits under your heel and arch—the parts of your foot that need support most. Carbon gives the insole strength without requiring a thick or bulky design.

The spring keeps it flexible

The carbon is shaped to act like a spring. It flexes when you step down, follows your foot as you move forward, and returns to its shape when you lift your foot.

It helps distribute the load

Instead of leaving your foot to carry the full load alone, the spring is designed to help distribute pressure through the structure as you walk.

It supports only what needs support

The three-quarter shape stops before the forefoot. Your heel and arch receive support, while your forefoot and toes remain free to move naturally.

It moves instead of pushing back

A rigid insole holds the foot in one position. CarboSteps moves with the foot, providing support throughout the step without creating the same hard, obstructive feeling.

Find your fit
What exactly am I buying?+

A full left-and-right pair of three-quarter supports built around the Carbon Motion System™: two carbon-fiber blades that sit under the heel and arch, bend under your weight and straighten again as you step off. The forefoot stays uncovered.

Will they fit my shoes?+

They are made for closed shoes with a removable or reasonably roomy footbed. Because every shoe has its own internal shape, very tight or low-volume shoes may not take them. Since the forefoot stays uncovered, they take up less room than a full-length insole.

Does carbon fiber feel hard?+

Carbon is used here as a spring, not as a stiff plate — the blades are meant to move under you. It feels firmer underfoot than foam, and most people notice the difference in the first few days. Comfort is personal, so read the return terms at checkout before you order.

Why do you need my weight?+

Because weight is what loads the spring. A blade that flexes properly under 130 lb is the wrong blade at 220 lb. Asking is the whole point — it’s the question a shelf insole never gets to ask.

Can I move them between shoes?+

Yes, between any compatible closed shoes. What matters is that they sit flat and secure and that the shoe has the internal room for them.

What’s included for $299?+

One full pair — left and right — built to your shoe size and body weight. $299 USD for the pair. Shipping, taxes and any additional charges are shown at checkout before payment.

Why is this a pre-order?+

Because this is the first production run. Ordering now reserves a pair from it at $299 and gives us the size and weight to build it to. The delivery estimate, total charge, cancellation terms and return policy are all set out at checkout before you pay.

Is this a medical device?+

No. CarboSteps is an everyday foot-support product. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it does not replace advice from a clinician. If you are being treated for a foot condition, ask them first.

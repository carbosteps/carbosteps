Our story

Born on the Côte d'Azur.

CarboSteps started in the French Riviera with one man who was determined to find a cure for his foot pain.

01 — The origin

Born from pain.

2022, on the French Riviera. Franck's days start early — pruning, planting, the quiet physical work of a gardener under the Mediterranean sun. By evening, he becomes someone else entirely: an artist and designer, hands in material, chasing the physics of things that bend, flex, and hold their shape.

That same year, Franck is diagnosed with a heel spur. Since 2019, he'd been living with a foot pain that grew little by little — small aches he brushed off, easy to ignore between long days in the garden. He never really took care of his feet, not until the pain became a real problem.

He went from one round of shockwave therapy to another. None of it gave him a lasting answer. He tried off-the-shelf insoles too — most didn't work at all, and the ones that did, only helped for a while before the material gave out. In his line of work, on his feet from sunrise to sundown, everything wore out faster than it was built to. Relief was never more than temporary.

“Foam absorbs energy and loses it. Carbon fiber stores it and gives it back.”

Franck, Co-founder & Designer

But Franck is an artist and a designer — someone with an intimate understanding of materials, how they behave, how they age, how they fail. He knew immediately what foam and plastic couldn't give him: foam doesn't last, and plastic loses its strength and form fast. Carbon fiber — the material of aerospace and motorsport — offered something neither ever could: resistance and elasticity that don't degrade.

So he decided to solve it himself. What began as sketches at his kitchen table turned into prototypes, refined with the guidance of a physiotherapist who helped him understand exactly where — and how — the foot needed support. Three years of iteration later, Franck had built something genuinely new: a carbon fiber sole that unloads the foot from pressure, stores the kinetic energy of each step, and releases it in sync with the natural gait — relieving the entire posterior kinetic chain, from foot to spine.

The pain lifted. And this time, it stayed away.

02 — The meeting

A new approach to comfort.

It was in July 2025, in Antibes, on the Côte d'Azur — the sun-drenched stretch of southern France, home to the Monaco Grand Prix and the Cannes Film Festival — that Bruno and Franck crossed paths.

When Bruno first saw the insole, his instinct was doubt. It looked hard — rigid even. Nothing about its appearance suggested comfort. But the moment he put it on, something unexpected happened. The firmness he feared wasn't there. In its place was something he hadn't felt before: the elasticity of rebound, a sole that didn't just cushion the foot but accompanied its movement. And then something else: his back felt lighter. The difference was subtle but real — and impossible to ignore.

Franck hadn't just made a better insole. He had replaced the softness of the material with the elasticity of its rebound — and in doing so, invented an entirely new approach to comfort.

Franck — Co-founder & Designer

Designer and artist by training, inventor by necessity. Franck built the first CarboSteps prototype in his living room after years of searching for a lasting solution to his own chronic heel pain.

Bruno — Co-founder & CEO

Entrepreneur and business builder. Bruno recognized the potential of Franck's invention and joined forces to turn a personal solution into a global product.

03 — The venture

Built for America.

Bruno was intrigued — but also skeptical. So they sought professional opinions. What emerged from those conversations was clear: CarboSteps doesn't claim to solve medical problems. What it does — measurably and consistently — is improve walking comfort and posture by relieving the posterior kinetic chain and accompanying the natural movement of the foot. The foot works less hard. The body stands taller.

They filed for a patent — not just for the insole, but for a broader vision: to make this carbon fiber architecture the centerpiece of an entirely new shoe sole, worn two ways — as a standalone insole or built into a shoe designed around it from the ground up.

The choice to launch in the United States was deliberate. Europe has its traditions — and its reluctance. America is where bold ideas find their audience, where new approaches to old problems get the platform they deserve. CarboSteps LLC was incorporated, and the journey began.
